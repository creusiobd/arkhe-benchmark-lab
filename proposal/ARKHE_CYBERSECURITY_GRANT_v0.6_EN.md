## Purpose

Request USD 10,000 to execute a preregistered, independently custodied defensive
study of trajectory-aware agent-security controls.

## Current evidence

- The public repository contains working SDKs, contracts, deterministic generation,
  evaluation isolation, OpenAI integration, telemetry, CI, and v0.5 reproducibility.
- v0.5 was inspected during development and remains `reproducibility_only`.
- No production effectiveness, external generalization, or confirmatory superiority
  is claimed.
- v0.6 has no dataset or results. Its design-stage contract prohibits confirmatory
  language until the protocol and sealed test manifest are frozen.

## Funded study

- 200 independently authored synthetic trajectories, 50 per family;
- four new families: persistent memory poisoning, cross-agent delegation confusion,
  skill/MCP supply-chain compromise, and authorization/identity drift;
- one primary comparator: event-level `gpt-6.1-sol` with Structured Outputs;
- one primary endpoint: paired timely-classification correctness;
- 15-percentage-point minimum relevant effect;
- 83.64% exact power at the predeclared 50% discordance design scenario;
- exact McNemar primary test, Wilson intervals, and full operational telemetry;
- publication of positive, null, and negative results.

## Independence gate

Before freeze, the project must name an independent case author/test custodian and
a protocol approver, complete the sensitivity analysis, and record the sealed
test-manifest SHA-256. Without those requirements, no confirmatory execution occurs.
